FAQ

Questions before you buy

Which software do I need?

The templates are PPTX files made for Microsoft PowerPoint. Morph transitions play in PowerPoint 2019, 2021 and Microsoft 365. Other apps can open the file, but the transitions may not play.

Do I need to install any fonts?

No. Every template uses fonts that ship with Microsoft Office, so the layout stays the same on any computer.

How much does a template cost?

Less than a cup of coffee. You get all 50 slides, Morph transitions and editable charts in one file. The exact price is shown on each marketplace at checkout, in your local currency.

How do I receive the file?

Buy from any marketplace listed above. The store gives you the download after payment.

Can I try before buying?

Yes. Download the free 5-slide sample above. It mixes five styles so you can test the layouts and Morph in your own PowerPoint.

Can I remove the MEGGYSTUDIO credit?

In the paid templates, yes. The "Powered by MEGGYSTUDIO" line is a normal text box. The free sample keeps its copyright mark.

Photos in our templates and on this site come from Unsplash, used under the Unsplash License. Thank you to the photographers whose work appears on this page:

Can I request a custom template?

Yes. Use the contact form above and tell us the topic and mood you need. Popular requests become new templates.
