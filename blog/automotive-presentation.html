How to Make an Automotive Presentation: Car Launches, EV Startups and Dealers

Car presentations sell design, performance and trust. How to structure an automotive deck for a model launch, an EV startup pitch or a dealership proposal.

Automotive presentations have a head start: cars are beautiful. The risk is letting the photos carry everything and forgetting the numbers people actually decide on, like range, price and delivery dates.

The short version
  • Photos create desire, numbers close it.
  • Three specs, big; the rest in an appendix.
  • Show the lineup by type of driver.

01Start with design

A big, clean photo of the car and a short line about what it was designed for. Then a blueprint or side profile to show the engineering behind it.

02Three specs, big

Range, acceleration, price. Or whichever three your buyer cares about most. Show them large and save the full spec sheet for the appendix.

Instead of

Full spec table with 24 rows

Try

520 km range. 0 to 100 in 4.1s. From $54,900.

03The lineup

If there are trims or models, show them side by side with one line each: who it's for.

04The market (for investor decks)

For an EV or mobility startup, show the market size and your slice of it, then unit economics: cost per car and margin.

05Pricing and team

Trims with prices, then the people behind the car. For dealers, swap the team for your service and sales staff.

Let the photos create desire, and the numbers close the deal.

06FAQ

What should a car launch presentation include?

The model and its design story, key specs, the lineup or trims, pricing, the target driver, and launch dates.

How should I present car specs?

Pick the three or four specs your audience cares about, such as range, 0 to 100 time and price, and show them large. Put the full spec sheet in an appendix.

What do investors want in an EV startup deck?

The market, your technology or advantage, production plan, unit economics, team and funding needs.
