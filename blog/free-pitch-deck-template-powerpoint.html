What's inside the free template

The sample mixes slides from different MEGGYSTUDIO styles, so you can see the range in one file:

  1. Cover: a confident opening slide in the Corporate style.
  2. Section divider: a bold divider from K-Pop Bento to split your story into chapters.
  3. Dashboard: a data-heavy content slide from Cyber Tech with stats and charts.
  4. Team: a team slide from Racing with photo cards and roles.
  5. Closing: a thank-you slide from Retro Game to end on a memorable note.

Why start with a template at all?

  • Structure: you start from proven slide types instead of a blank page.
  • Consistency: colors, spacing and type are already aligned, so the deck looks designed.
  • Time: you spend your hours on the story and the numbers, not on nudging boxes.
  • Motion: Morph is already applied, so your deck feels smooth with zero animation work.

How to use it in 4 steps

  1. Open it in PowerPoint 2019, 2021, 2024 or Microsoft 365 to see Morph in action.
  2. Replace the text. Click into any text box and type. The styles stay intact.
  3. Swap the photos. Right-click a picture, choose Change Picture, and pick your own.
  4. Match your brand. Select a shape, open Shape Fill, and use your brand color. Repeat for the key accents.
Keep the Morph working: when you edit, change objects instead of deleting and redrawing them. Morph pairs objects that exist on both slides. More in our Morph guide.

FAQ

Is the free pitch deck template really free?

Yes. The 5-slide sample is free to download from meggystudio.com, no sign-up needed. You can use it for your own presentations.

Does the free template work in Google Slides or Keynote?

It's made for Microsoft PowerPoint. Google Slides and Keynote can open the file, but the Morph transitions won't play and some layouts may shift.

Can I sell or share the free template?

No. You can use it in your own presentations, but you can't resell or redistribute the template file itself.
