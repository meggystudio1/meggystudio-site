Aesthetic PowerPoint Templates

Aesthetic decks are about mood. Soft colour, good photos, a little texture and type that feels personal. They are popular with creators, students, small brands and anyone presenting something close to their heart.

To keep an aesthetic deck readable, stick to the template palette, use photos with a similar tone and keep text short. One beautiful slide with one clear idea beats a pretty slide nobody can read.

FAQ

What makes a presentation look aesthetic?

A limited palette, consistent photo tone, plenty of space and one or two typefaces. Templates handle most of this for you.

Can I use aesthetic templates for school?

Yes. They work well for personal projects, book reports and creative subjects.
