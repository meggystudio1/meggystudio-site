How to choose a lifestyle or seasonal template

Lifestyle templates cover travel, fitness, food, wellness and seasonal campaigns. For seasonal decks, pick the template for the moment and start early: campaigns are usually approved weeks ahead. Christmas Bento, Chinese New Year 2027 and Ramadan Layered are made for those moments.

For businesses like gyms, cafes and travel agencies, look for pricing, schedule and photo-heavy slides. Our guides to seasonal presentations and Christmas presentation ideas help you plan the content.

FAQ

Can I reuse a seasonal template every year?

Yes. Change the year on the cover and the dates on the timeline; the layouts work for every season.

Do lifestyle templates include photos?

They include placeholder photos you can replace by right-clicking and choosing Change Picture.

Which template is best for a fitness business?

Gym Fitness, Run Club and Padel Club include class schedules, membership pricing and coach slides.
