Five colours is all a deck needs

The generator gives you five roles, not five random colours. Background and surface carry most of the slide. Text has to pass contrast against both. Accent is for the one thing you want people to look at, such as a key number or a button. Accent 2 is a backup for charts that need a second series. If everything is accent, nothing is.

Check the contrast badges

Text should reach at least 4.5:1 against its background so people at the back of the room can read it. Accents used for large numbers or shapes only need about 3:1. Projectors wash colours out, so when a badge says low, darken the text or lighten the background before you present.

Put the palette into PowerPoint

  1. Open the Design tab, then Variants, then Colors, then Customize Colors.
  2. Paste Text into Text/Background Dark 1 and Background into Text/Background Light 1.
  3. Put Accent and Accent 2 into Accent 1 and Accent 2, then save the colour set with a name.

FAQ

What is a good colour scheme for a presentation?

One dark and one light neutral for text and background, plus one strong accent. Keep text contrast at 4.5:1 or higher.

How do I use my brand colour in a presentation?

Use it as the accent, not the background, unless it is very dark or very light. Pick neutrals that are tinted towards the brand colour so the deck feels connected.
