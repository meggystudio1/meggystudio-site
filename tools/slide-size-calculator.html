Slide size calculator

Convert any screen, post or paper size into the exact PowerPoint slide size, with the export resolution and steps to set it up.

Set it in PowerPoint

    16:9

    How pixels turn into slide sizes

    PowerPoint measures slides in inches or centimetres, not pixels. The default widescreen slide is 13.333 by 7.5 inches, which matches a 1920 by 1080 pixel Full HD screen at 144 pixels per inch. When you enter a pixel size, the calculator keeps the slide 7.5 inches on its short side, like PowerPoint's own presets, and sets the long side to match your ratio. What matters for screens is the shape: get the ratio right and the slide fills the display without black bars.

    LED walls and unusual screens

    Event screens are often very wide, such as 3840 by 1080 or 5760 by 1080. Ask the production team for the exact pixel size, enter it here, and set your slide size before you design anything. Changing the size later stretches photos and pushes text out of place. Our event-ready templates keep the important content in the centre so they survive cropping.

    Making social posts in PowerPoint

    PowerPoint is a quick way to make carousels and stories. Pick the square, portrait or story preset, design one post per slide, then use File, Export and choose PNG to save every slide as an image. If you want slides with motion, export a video instead; our guide on exporting PowerPoint to video walks through it.

    FAQ

    What size is a PowerPoint slide in pixels?

    The default widescreen slide is 13.333 by 7.5 inches. Exported at the usual setting it becomes 1920 by 1080 pixels, a 16:9 ratio.

    Should I use 16:9 or 4:3?

    Use 16:9 for almost every modern screen, TV and laptop. Use 4:3 only if you know the projector or the venue needs it.

    What is the largest slide size in PowerPoint?

    56 by 56 inches. For bigger prints, design at a smaller size with the same ratio and export at a higher resolution.
