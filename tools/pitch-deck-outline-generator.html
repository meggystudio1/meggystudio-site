Pitch deck outline generator

Choose your business type, stage and audience. You get the slides to include, in order, what to put on each one and a headline example you can rewrite.

    Why the outline changes with your answers

    There is no single correct pitch deck. A pre-launch hardware startup has no traction slide to show, but it does need to prove people want the product and that it can be made at a sensible cost. A marketplace lives or dies on unit economics. A film pitch needs a story and an audience before it needs a market size. The generator starts from a proven order for each business type, then removes or adds slides for your stage and audience.

    Write headlines, not labels

    Every slide comes with a headline example. "Traction" tells the room what the slide is about; "40% month-on-month growth since launch" tells them what to think. Investors skim. If they only read your slide titles, they should still get the whole story. Read more in our pitch deck structure guide and the list of pitch deck red flags investors notice.

    From outline to slides

    Copy the outline into your speaker notes, then pick a layout for each section. Every MEGGYSTUDIO template has 50 different layouts, including team, market, roadmap and chart slides, so you can map each line to a slide and delete the rest. The startup pitch deck collection is a good place to start.

    FAQ

    How many slides should a pitch deck have?

    About 10 to 15 for an investor meeting, 5 to 8 for a 3-minute demo day. Put extra detail in an appendix.

    What order should pitch deck slides go in?

    Usually problem, solution, why now, market, business model, traction, competition, team and the ask. Lead with your strongest proof if it is unusually good.

    Should I include financial projections at the idea stage?

    Keep them out of the main deck. Show validation and the milestones the money unlocks instead, and keep a simple model ready for questions.
